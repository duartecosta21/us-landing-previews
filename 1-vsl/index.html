The problem

Your ads are probably not the weak link.

Most agencies stop at the click. A lead that waits, gets a cold answer or never hears back is a lead you paid for and lost.

Slow first reply

The lead filled the form while on their phone. Hours later someone calls back, and they already booked somewhere else.

No real follow-up

One call attempt, one text, then silence. Most leads need several touches before they commit to a visit.

Booking friction

Back and forth over availability, forms that ask too much, no reminders. The appointment never makes it onto the calendar.
