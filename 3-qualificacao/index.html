The real leak

Most of your ad budget is lost after the click, not before it

You can have a good offer, good creative and a fair cost per lead, and still watch patients disappear. A lead who filled out a form at 9 pm is comparing three clinics by 9:15.

  • Slow first reply. The lead cools off while the form waits for Monday.
  • No follow-up. One call, one text, then silence.
  • Front desk as a bottleneck. Busy with patients in the chair, so new inquiries sit.
  • Booked, then gone. No reminders, so the consult is a no-show.

Sound familiar? "We tried ads and it did not work" or "an agency sent leads but nothing booked" are usually this problem, not an ads problem.

Questions

Before you apply

Who is this for?

Owners and decision makers of dental, med spa, plastic surgery and general medicine practices that already spend on ads. If you are not advertising yet, our free resource is a better first step.

Why do you ask about revenue and ad spend?

So we only book calls where we can actually help. It also lets us prepare the audit around your numbers instead of spending your time on basics.

What happens on the audit call?

We look at your ad account, your lead path and how inquiries are answered. You get specific findings. There is no pressure to continue.

How much does it cost?

Investment depends on your clinic and goals. We walk you through it on the call.

What is included?

Full Meta and Google ad management, landing pages built by us, creatives, a CRM set up or tuned, and two front desk coaching sessions a month.

I tried an agency before. Why would this be different?

Most agencies stop at the lead. We build around what happens after it, from the CRM to your front desk, and report on consults, not clicks.

Do you handle patient data safely?

We only work with inquiry details such as name, phone, email and treatment interest, never charts or medical records. Where HIPAA applies, we sign a Business Associate Agreement before touching any lead data.
