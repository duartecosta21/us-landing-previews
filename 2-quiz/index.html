Straight answers

How is the estimate calculated?

Leads lost = leads per month x an assumed loss rate for your reply time. Extra bookings = leads lost x your current booking rate. Value = extra bookings x your average ticket, before show-up and close rates. The loss rates are conservative, illustrative modeling assumptions, shown as a range. Your own data will differ.

Is this a guarantee of results?

No. It is a rough estimate to show where to look first. The free audit uses your real numbers.

What do I get on the free call?

A review of your ads and how your team handles a new lead, plus the two or three fixes most likely to matter. It is a 20-minute call, followed by a one-page written audit sent within 48 hours.

Do I have to replace my team or my ads?

No. We work with what you have and tell you honestly if the ads are fine and the gap is elsewhere.
