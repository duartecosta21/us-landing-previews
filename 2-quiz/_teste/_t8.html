How is the estimate calculated?

Leads lost = leads per month x an assumed loss rate for your reply time. Extra bookings = leads lost x your current booking rate. Value = extra bookings x your average ticket, before show-up and close rates. The loss rates are modeling assumptions, shown as a range. Your own data will differ.

Is this a guarantee of results?

No. It is a rough estimate to show where to look first. The free audit uses your real numbers.
